Privacy Policy

Floramere respects your privacy. We collect only information needed to process inquiries, newsletter signups and purchases.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Floramere does not store full payment card details.

Forms and newsletter

Contact forms may be processed by Formspree. Newsletter signups may be processed by MailerLite.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared only with service providers required to operate the website, process payments, manage forms, send newsletters or comply with legal obligations.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data through the contact form.
