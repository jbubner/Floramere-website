Shipping & Returns

Limited releases

Floramere signature pieces may be offered as limited release, pre-order or made-to-order items. Estimated production and shipping timelines will be communicated after purchase confirmation.

Shipping

Shipping options and costs may vary depending on destination. International customers may be responsible for import duties, customs charges or local taxes.

Returns

Because Floramere pieces may be limited release or made to order, returns may be limited unless an item arrives damaged or defective.

Damaged items

Please contact us within 7 days of delivery if your item arrives damaged or defective. Include your order information and photos of the issue.
